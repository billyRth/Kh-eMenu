# Brag Plan: KhMenu

## What is this app?
KhMenu puts a QR code on every restaurant table: diners scan, order from their phone and split the bill, and the order rings straight to the kitchen's phone. Made in Phnom Penh for Cambodian restaurants.

## The angle
A table-to-kitchen relay. One order travels from a QR sticker on Table 4, through a diner's phone, to a notification on the staff phone, then the video fans out the features restaurant owners actually care about. It's warm and local (dollars and riel, Fish Amok, "App កូនខ្មែរ"), not a generic SaaS reel.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A QR table tent labelled "Table 4" snaps into frame, the scan brackets lock onto it, and the line "Scan. Order. Done." lands word by word.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real diner menu (Sabay Kitchen card, Fish Amok / Lok Lak / Iced Coffee rows): three taps add dishes, the orange "View order · 3 · $14.75" bar slides up, and "Send order to kitchen" gets tapped.
- The staff phone rings: "New order #12 · Table 4 · 2× Fish Amok, 1× Iced Khmer Coffee · $14.75", with the order card showing "Start cooking".
- Four owner-facing features arrive one by one and hold: Split the bill · Dollars + riel · Call waiter & ask for bill · 6 menu styles (with the six theme swatches).

## Outro / punchline
KhMenu wordmark (Kh in orange) → "App កូនខ្មែរ" → "DM us for a free demo".

## User flow worth showing
Scan the table QR → add dishes and send the order → the staff phone gets the notification and starts cooking.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: warm Phnom Penh launch film, one order's trip from table to kitchen
- Interpretation: clean feature-card reveals and smooth slides, the brand's warm cream/orange palette, confident holds so every line is readable; no jokes, no chaos.
