Paper menu, or KhMenu? 📄 vs 📱

The paper menu: dishes sold out get written in by hand, prices get crossed out, and guests still wait to catch a waiter.
KhMenu: guests scan the QR code on the table, browse your menu with photos, tap to order — and the order lands on your staff's phone straight away.
Change a dish or a price once, and every guest sees it immediately.
