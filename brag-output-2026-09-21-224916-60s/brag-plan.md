# Brag Plan: KhMenu Staff — the update (60s cut)

## What is this app?
KhMenu ("App កូនខ្មែរ") is a QR menu and table-ordering app for Cambodian restaurants. Diners scan the table QR and order from their phone. Staff run the floor from the staff app: live orders, a table grid and an end-of-day report.

## The angle
The 24s "what's new in KhMenu Staff" update drop, stretched to 60s with more story and calmer holds rather than slower motion. One order is followed from a diner's phone to the staff board and then across two waiters' phones. After that come the six staff-app updates, each with 4–6s to breathe. It's distinct from video 1 (a vertical order-relay launch) and video 2 (a vertical owner sales pitch).

## Hook (0–2.45s)
Same as the 24s cut: orange, bell icon, "New in KhMenu Staff", "6 updates for your floor team", and real UI chips (ខ្មែរ · 中文 · 4 · Seated · Table 3 → Table 7 · Broken · CSV).

## Key moments
- **Diner opener:** the phone scans the Table 2 QR, and the menu opens in Khmer (English names underneath, $ + ៛ prices). The guest taps + three times on Fish Amok, which turns the button into the new − / n / + pill, then − once (3 → 2), then + on Iced Khmer Coffee. The order sheet ("ការកុម្ម៉ង់របស់អ្នក", $14.75 · 60,500៛) goes out with "ផ្ញើទៅផ្ទះបាយ", and the toast reads "ការកុម្ម៉ង់ #24 បានផ្ញើទៅផ្ទះបាយ!".
- **Staff board:** the app's chime (the real two-tone 880/1320 Hz sound, synthesized) and the bell rings. The toast "New order #24 · Table 2" appears, card #24 drops into New, and the Table 2 tile goes $10.00 → $24.75.
- **Two phones in sync:** Dara and Vanna both tap "Start cooking" on #24. Dara's tap lands first, so both phones move the card to Preparing, and Vanna's phone shows "Order #24 was already updated on another device". Vanna then marks it Served and Dara's phone follows.
- **The 6 updates:** the language flip EN → ខ្មែរ → 中文 → EN; seat Table 5 with 4 guests; move Table 3 → Table 7; **combine two busy tables** (Table 8 with its own $22.00 order joins Table 7, so Table 7 becomes $40.50, the Open bills card shows "2 orders" with Bopha $18.50 · Sokha $22.00, order #26's badge flips Table 8 → Table 7, and Table 8 keeps its 3 guests with "With Table 7"); mark Table 1 unavailable with "Broken"; Today tab with the Guests tile at 23, a "per guest" beat ($147.20 ÷ 23 guests = $6.40 each) and Download CSV.

## Outro
Orange: icon + "KhMenu Staff", "Update out now", "App កូនខ្មែរ · Made in Phnom Penh". Music fades to silence.

Unchanged from the 24s cut: #faf8f5 / #f3efe9, accent #c2410c, ink #292524, Plus Jakarta Sans + Kantumruy Pro + Microsoft YaHei, lucide icons from the project, and the bell app icon.

## Audio direction
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (109.96 BPM, 60.0s). The track runs the full length with no looping needed, and a volume-automation fade runs 58.3 → 59.9s.
- Beat grid: B(k) = 0.267 + 0.54565·k from the bundled cue preset. Every beat and cut lands on it (hook wipe B4, order lands B26, phones B33, updates B46, counts land B95, outro B103).
- SFX: select taps, switch on the language flips, the app's own chime on the new order, a drop on toasts and the "already updated" heads-up, keypresses on "Broken", chips on the count-up, a card slide on scene changes and the CSV, and a bell on the outro.
- Audio-reactive: none (keeps the UI text steady).
