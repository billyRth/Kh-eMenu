# Hyperframes Composition Brief: KhMenu Owner Pitch

## Objective
A problem → solution → CTA sales video for Cambodian restaurant owners (Facebook ad).

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output-owner-pitch/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output-owner-pitch/brag-owner-pitch.mp4` (+ poster `brag-owner-pitch.jpg`)
- Format: vertical, 1080x1920
- Duration: 31.1 seconds

## Source Material
- Files read: HANDOVER.md, src/pages/admin/DailyReport.tsx, src/pages/admin/OrdersBoard.tsx, src/lib/i18n.ts, src/pages/LandingPage.tsx, brag-output/ (the first video, for brand and style)
- Copy that must appear verbatim:
  - App កូនខ្មែរ
  - Total sales / Profit / After food costs (Today report labels)
  - Free / Eating / Calling / Wants bill (table grid states)
- No prices for KhMenu itself (pricing is not final)

## Creative Direction
- Dark stone problem scenes with red pain accents, then an orange circle reveal into the warm cream/orange brand. This looks different from the first video's cream-only table-tent opening.
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, stating subscription prices

## Visual Identity
- Local fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans 500/700/800, Kantumruy Pro 700 (Khmer). Khmer lines use a line-height of 1.5 or more so the subscripts aren't clipped.
- Palette: #1c1917 / #292524 dark, #dc2626 pain red, #c2410c orange, #faf8f5 cream
