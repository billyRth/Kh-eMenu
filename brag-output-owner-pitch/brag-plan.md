# Brag Plan: KhMenu — Owner Pitch

## What is this app?
KhMenu ("App កូនខ្មែរ") puts a QR code on every restaurant table. Diners scan, order from their phone in Khmer, English or Chinese with USD + riel prices; staff get every order live with push notifications, a table grid and bill split, and the owner gets an end-of-day sales and profit report.

## The angle
A sales pitch to Cambodian restaurant owners, cut for Facebook ads. Problem → solution → CTA. It opens dark and messy (paper menus, running waiters, lost order slips, "Profit today: ???"), then an orange circle bursts open into KhMenu and the video turns warm and calm. The first video followed one order's trip. This one is about the owner's day, and it ends on the profit number.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Dark screen, a stained paper menu tilts in with order slips scattered around it, and the Khmer question "នៅតែប្រើម៉ឺនុយក្រដាស?" lands, followed by "Still running your restaurant on paper?"

## Key moments (the middle)
- Three pain cards stack up: waiters running back and forth, missed and mixed-up orders, no idea what you made today ("Profit today: ???").
- The turn: the pain cards drop away and an orange circle opens to KhMenu + App កូនខ្មែរ.
- Diners order themselves: the phone menu flips EN → ខ្មែរ → 中文 with $ + ៛ prices.
- Staff see everything live: a "New order · Table 4" push drops, then the 8-table grid changes state (Eating / Calling / Wants bill) and a bill split pops up.
- Owner closes the day: the Today report counts up Total sales and Profit.

## Outro / punchline
Cream background, KhMenu wordmark, App កូនខ្មែរ, then the CTA "Try the live demo", with "Message us to get set up" under it. No prices.

## User flow worth showing
Diner changes language on the menu → the order reaches the staff board live → the owner reads the end-of-day profit.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store (with a problem/solution ad structure)
- Creative direction: Facebook ad for Cambodian restaurant owners. Dark, cluttered problem, then a warm, calm, orange solution.
- Interpretation: short, readable lines with confident holds. Numbers and UI instead of adjectives. No jokes.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 31.1s (the user asked for 25–35s, overriding the 15–25s default)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #faf8f5 cream; problem scenes on #1c1917 stone-black; turn scene orange #ea580c → #c2410c → #7c2d12
- Accent: #c2410c; "pain" red #dc2626 used only in the problem scenes
- Text: #292524
- Display font: Plus Jakarta Sans 800; Khmer: Kantumruy Pro 700; Chinese: system YaHei fallback
- Strongest visual element: the Today report with the profit number; the table grid

## Share copy (draft)
Still running your restaurant on paper? With KhMenu, diners scan the table QR and order in Khmer, English or Chinese, your staff get every order live, and you see your profit before you close. App កូនខ្មែរ 🇰🇭 Try the live demo or message us to get set up.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 0–3.27s
Stone-black bg. A tilted paper menu with a coffee ring and scattered order slips. "នៅតែប្រើម៉ឺនុយក្រដាស?" at 0.56, "Still running your restaurant on paper?" at 1.09, then hold.
Transition: hard cut on the beat.

### Scene 2 — The mess — 3.27–8.74s
Label "Every service, the same problems". Three dark cards with red icons arrive at 3.82 / 4.91 / 6.00:
1. Waiters running back and forth (table → kitchen → table, all night)
2. Missed & mixed-up orders (handwritten slip "T4: 2 amok… or 3?")
3. No idea what you made today ("Profit today: ???" jitters)
All three hold until about 8.2s, then drop away.

### Scene 3 — The turn — 8.74–11.46s
An orange circle opens from the center on the 8.74 strong cue. The KhMenu icon and wordmark land, then "App កូនខ្មែរ" and "QR menu & table ordering".

### Scene 4 — Diners order themselves — 11.46–16.93s
Caption "Diners scan & order themselves". Phone menu with language pills: EN at the start, ខ្មែរ at 12.55, 中文 at 13.64. Dish names change (Fish Amok / អាម៉ុកត្រី / 鱼肉阿莫克). Prices stay "$6.50 · 26,700៛". The chip "$ + ៛ prices" lands at 14.73.

### Scene 5 — Staff see it live — 16.93–22.37s
Caption "Every order, live on staff phones". A push notification "New order · Table 4" drops at 17.47. Table grid T1–T8: T6 → Calling at 18.56, T2 → Wants bill at 19.66, then a bill-split sheet "Split 3 ways · $8.40 each" at 20.75.

### Scene 6 — Know your profit — 22.37–26.74s
Caption "Know your profit before you close". Today report: Total sales counts to $486.50 (+12% vs day before) and Profit to $212.40 ("After food costs"), then Orders 64 and a best-seller bar for Fish Amok.

### Scene 7 — CTA — 26.74–31.1s
Cream. Icon + KhMenu, "App កូនខ្មែរ · Made in Phnom Penh", CTA pill "Try the live demo" at 27.30, and "Message us to get set up" at 27.83. Music fades out.
